01 — ABOUT

Building my journey one project at a time.

I'm Ambi Rajan, an aspiring Data Analyst passionate about exploring data, uncovering meaningful insights and using technology to solve real-world problems.

My interests include data analysis, data visualization and transforming raw information into structured insights. I work with tools and technologies including Microsoft Excel, SQL, Python, R Programming, Power BI and Tableau.

I have developed hands-on projects involving retail sales analysis, HR attrition analysis, food sales performance analysis and interactive dashboards. Through these projects, I explore data preparation, analytical techniques, visualization and reporting using practical datasets.

My creative interests include graphic design, digital editing, drawing, painting and 3D modelling. I enjoy combining analytical thinking and creativity to build meaningful projects.

I believe in continuous learning, practical application and turning ideas into useful solutions. My goal is to grow in Data Analytics and contribute to projects that create value.
